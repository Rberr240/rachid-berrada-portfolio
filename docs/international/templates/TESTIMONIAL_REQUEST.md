# Testimonial & Referral Request Templates

Internal reference. Used at the TESTIMONIAL and REFERRAL stages of [../SALES_PLAYBOOK.md](../SALES_PLAYBOOK.md), after a project is DELIVERED and final payment/sign-off is complete.

Keep both requests short, specific, and low-friction — the goal is to make it easy to say yes in under two minutes, not to ask for a favor that requires effort to draft. Never fabricate or edit the substance of a testimonial received; never publish one without the client's clear agreement (per the no-fabrication rule in [../MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md](../MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md)).

---

## 1. Testimonial request

**Timing:** send a few days to a couple of weeks after delivery, once the client has had a real chance to use what was built — not immediately at handoff.

### English

Hi [First Name], hope [the website / the system / [specific deliverable]] has been working well for you so far.

If you've got two minutes, would you be open to sharing a short line or two about your experience working together — what the problem was, and how it's helped? It would genuinely help other businesses like yours understand what to expect.

No pressure at all if now isn't a good time — and totally fine if you'd rather I ask again later.

*(Optional add-on if a public display is planned: "If you're comfortable with it, I'd also love to include your name and [Business Name] alongside it — just let me know either way.")*

### Français

Bonjour [Prénom], j'espère que [le site / le système / [livrable spécifique]] fonctionne bien de votre côté depuis la mise en ligne.

Si vous avez deux minutes, seriez-vous d'accord pour partager quelques lignes sur votre expérience de collaboration — quel était le problème, et en quoi cela vous a aidé ? Cela aiderait vraiment d'autres entreprises comme la vôtre à savoir à quoi s'attendre.

Aucune pression si ce n'est pas le bon moment — et pas de souci si vous préférez que je vous le redemande plus tard.

*(Optionnel, si une publication publique est prévue : « Si vous êtes à l'aise avec cela, j'aimerais aussi mentionner votre nom et [Nom de l'entreprise] à côté — dites-moi simplement ce que vous en pensez. »)*

### If no response

Send at most one polite reminder after about a week. Do not follow up further — a testimonial is a bonus, not owed by the client.

> "Hi [First Name], just following up in case my last message got buried — totally fine either way, just wanted to check. Hope everything's going well with [deliverable]!"

## 2. Referral request

**Timing:** after the client has expressed genuine satisfaction (in the testimonial, in conversation, or through continued contact) — not automatically bundled with every testimonial ask.

### English

Hi [First Name], really glad [deliverable] has been useful for you. One small thing — if you happen to know anyone else who might be dealing with a similar situation (whether that's [a website / automation / a specific type of problem]), I'd really appreciate an introduction.

Happy to give you a short line you could forward if that makes it easier — just let me know.

### Français

Bonjour [Prénom], je suis vraiment content que [livrable] vous soit utile. Une petite chose — si vous connaissez quelqu'un d'autre qui pourrait être confronté à une situation similaire (que ce soit [un site web / une automatisation / un type de problème spécifique]), une mise en relation serait vraiment appréciée.

Je peux vous préparer un court message que vous pourriez transférer si cela facilite les choses — dites-moi simplement.

---

## 3. Process notes

- Ask for the testimonial first; only raise the referral once satisfaction is clearly established (do not combine both asks into a single message by default).
- Keep both requests short enough to read and act on from a phone in under a minute.
- Log the outcome of each request (received, declined, no response) and close out the TESTIMONIAL and REFERRAL pipeline stages regardless of outcome, per [../SALES_PLAYBOOK.md](../SALES_PLAYBOOK.md).
