Premium business websites, landing pages, responsive experiences, client portals, dashboards, business web platforms, conversion-focused forms, booking/contact systems.

**Value proposition:** Modern digital experiences that make businesses look professional and convert visitors into customers.
**Internal reference range:** $700 – $3,000+

### Pillar 2 — AI & Business Automation
Lead automation, CRM workflows, email workflows, AI-powered workflows, Make.com integrations, business operations automation, intelligent customer flows, repetitive-process elimination.

**Value proposition:** Automate manual work and connect business tools into reliable workflows.
**Internal reference range:** $800 – $5,000+

### Pillar 3 — Custom Software
Custom business applications, internal systems, SaaS-style applications, operational dashboards, secure client areas, administration systems, tailored digital products.

**Value proposition:** Software designed around the company's real workflow instead of forcing the company into generic software.
**Internal reference range:** $2,000 – $10,000+

### Pillar 4 — AI Systems Engineering
AI agents, RAG, internal AI assistants, multi-agent workflows, AI system architecture, model integrations, intelligent business tools, orchestration.

**Value proposition:** Advanced AI systems designed for real operational use.
**Internal reference range:** $3,000 – $15,000+

## 2. Public pricing strategy

Do not publish these ranges prominently. Use **"Starting from"** framing for the two most approachable, well-scoped offers (Launch Website, Business Website Pro) and **"Request a quote"** for everything scope-dependent (automation, custom software, AI). This keeps the site accessible to smaller prospects while protecting margin on larger, variable-scope work.

## 4. Pricing philosophy

Price on **value + complexity + risk + time + support**, not hourly rate alone.

- **Fixed projects** (Launch Website, Business Website Pro): quote a fixed price after a short discovery call confirms scope matches the productized offer. Any scope beyond the standard inclusions moves to a custom quote.
- **Milestone-based projects** (Custom Software, AI Systems Engineering, larger automation): break into discovery → build → launch milestones, each with a payment tied to a deliverable (see [SALES_PLAYBOOK.md](./SALES_PLAYBOOK.md)).
- **Discovery phase:** for ambiguous scope, offer a short paid discovery engagement that produces a fixed-price proposal, rather than guessing a number up front.
- **Retainers:** for ongoing automation support or iterative AI work, offer a monthly retainer once the initial system is delivered and stable — no fixed number published here; set per client based on system complexity and support SLA.
- **Maintenance / automation support:** small monthly fee for monitoring a delivered automation (e.g. the lead pipeline pattern) plus a per-incident rate for larger changes.

Do not invent market-rate claims ("agencies charge $X") — reference only Rachid's own internal ranges above.
