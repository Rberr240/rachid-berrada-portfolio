# 90-Day International Launch Roadmap

Internal reference. See [MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md](./MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md) for canonical facts this document must stay consistent with, and [KPI_SCORECARD.md](./KPI_SCORECARD.md) for the weekly metrics used to track progress against this plan.

> **Everything with a number attached in this document is a target, not a guarantee.** Real client acquisition timelines vary with market conditions, offer-market fit, and effort consistency. These numbers exist to give the plan a shape and a way to tell, honestly, whether the international push is on track — not as a promise of outcome.

## Days 1–30: Foundation

Goal: get the brand, offers, and portfolio internationally credible, get profiles live, and start generating real signal from the market.

- **Finalize brand.** Lock positioning statement, commercial promise, and differentiator (see [BRAND_SYSTEM.md](./BRAND_SYSTEM.md)) as final — stop iterating on core messaging so everything downstream (content, outreach, proposals) can be written against a stable identity.
- **Optimize profiles.** Create and configure Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, and (optional) X per [MANUAL_ACTIONS.md](./MANUAL_ACTIONS.md) — consistent handle, bio, and profile link across all platforms.
- **Internationalize the portfolio.** English-first public site experience live, French preserved and reachable (see BRAND_SYSTEM.md §5), verified case studies only (no fabricated scale — see MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md §4), pricing/offers framing matching [OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md](./OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md) §2.
- **Publish first content.** Get the first batch of content live across platforms (see [CONTENT_ENGINE.md](./CONTENT_ENGINE.md), [content/SHORTS_001_030.md](./content/SHORTS_001_030.md), [content/LINKEDIN_POSTS_001_010.md](./content/LINKEDIN_POSTS_001_010.md)) — consistency matters more than volume in this window.
- **Begin outbound.** Start structured prospecting using [OUTBOUND_PLAYBOOK.md](./OUTBOUND_PLAYBOOK.md) — first target lists identified, first messages sent.
- **Validate acquisition channels.** Track which of content, outbound, and referral produce the earliest signal (replies, conversations) so effort can shift toward what's actually working rather than assumptions.

**By day 30, target state:** brand and offers finalized and consistent everywhere; all social profiles live; portfolio internationalized and QA'd; first content batch published; outbound sequences running; enough weekly data in the [KPI_SCORECARD.md](./KPI_SCORECARD.md) to see which channels are responding.

## Days 31–60: Consistency and volume

Goal: turn the foundation into a repeatable weekly motion, and start having real sales conversations.

- **Consistent content cadence.** Publishing on a fixed, sustainable schedule across the primary platforms (see CONTENT_ENGINE.md for cadence recommendations) — no gaps long enough to lose algorithmic momentum or audience trust.
- **100–200 targeted prospects.** Cumulative total of qualified, targeted prospects identified and added to the outbound pipeline since day 1.
- **Regular conversations.** Multiple concurrent, active back-and-forth conversations (inbound and outbound combined) each week, not just replies.
- **Initial calls.** First discovery calls held (see [templates/DISCOVERY_CALL.md](./templates/DISCOVERY_CALL.md)) — even a small number here is a meaningful milestone, since it validates that outreach + content are producing conversations that convert to a call.
- **Improve offers based on real response.** Use actual objections, questions, and drop-off points from these first conversations to sharpen [OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md](./OFFERS_AND_PRICING.md) framing and the proposal template — this is the point where real market feedback should start changing messaging, not guesswork.

**By day 60, target state:** content cadence is boring/reliable rather than bursty; 100–200 cumulative targeted prospects contacted; several live conversations at any given time; a handful of discovery calls completed; at least one concrete offer/messaging adjustment made based on real prospect feedback.

## Days 61–90: Convert and compound

Goal: convert the pipeline built in the first 60 days into paying clients, and establish the repeatable system for the next quarter.

**Targets for day 90 (explicitly targets, not guarantees):**

- **~100 pieces of content** published cumulatively across platforms since day 1.
- **~300 targeted prospects** contacted cumulatively via outbound.
- **~30 commercial conversations** — real back-and-forth exchanges about a potential project, cumulative.
- **~10 calls** — discovery calls or sales calls held, cumulative.
- **3–5 paying international clients** — the primary business outcome this roadmap is built to produce. "International" here means outside Rachid's immediate local network, consistent with the primary launch markets in BRAND_SYSTEM.md §5 (France, Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, Morocco premium B2B, UAE/Dubai).

Supporting work in this window:

- Close the loop on the sales process for won deals (see [SALES_PLAYBOOK.md](./SALES_PLAYBOOK.md)) through to delivery and testimonial collection ([templates/TESTIMONIAL_REQUEST.md](./templates/TESTIMONIAL_REQUEST.md)) — a client delivered without a testimonial request is a missed compounding opportunity per the CRM V2 pipeline design (`DELIVERED` → `TESTIMONIAL` → `REFERRAL` in [CRM_V2_SPEC.md](./CRM_V2_SPEC.md)).
- Review the full 90 days of [KPI_SCORECARD.md](./KPI_SCORECARD.md) data to identify which channel(s) actually produced wins, and weight the next quarter's effort accordingly.
- Revisit [INTERNATIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md](./INTERNATIONAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md) — anything still unchecked at day 90 should be explicitly triaged (still needed / no longer relevant / deferred with a reason).

## How to use this roadmap

- Review progress against it weekly using the KPI scorecard, not just at the 30/60/90 checkpoints — course-correct early rather than discovering a gap at day 89.
- If a target is missed, the useful question is *why* (wrong channel, wrong offer, insufficient volume, weak follow-up), not whether to abandon the plan — see [KPI_SCORECARD.md](./KPI_SCORECARD.md)'s per-channel and conversion-rate breakdowns for that diagnosis.
- Nothing in this roadmap authorizes changes to the production lead pipeline, CRM, or analytics implementation beyond what's already approved — those remain gated per [CRM_V2_SPEC.md](./CRM_V2_SPEC.md) and [UTM_TRACKING_V2.md](./UTM_TRACKING_V2.md).
